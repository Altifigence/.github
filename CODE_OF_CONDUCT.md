# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, explain technical
disagreements, and welcome contributors with different experience and backgrounds.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam and disclosure of
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions
or restrict participation. Use GitHub's **Report abuse** action on the relevant
content for confidential conduct reports. Report software vulnerabilities through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public discussions.

These expectations apply to this organization's public repositories and their
issues, pull requests and discussions. They do not change any software license.
